What it is

The piece is a stack of 14 shots, each a pure function of time, so any frame can be rendered in any order. Everything on screen is drawn procedurally: the pixel-art icons, the thermal-camera head and hand, the handwriting scribbles and the type. No footage from the reference is used. The shots, in order:

  1. how do you kinetic type opener
  2. Typing line with handwritten scribbles
  3. Giant cyan sparkle with floating pixel icons
  4. Thermal head profile “you dont.” / “you just show it.”
  5. 3D ring of 12 original pixel-art icons and a bouncing ink ball
  6. Word cards “action.” “intention.” “curiosity.”
  7. Ink scatter
  8. Thermal hand “through ones own ability to”
  9. L-O-V-E finale with red pen loops

How it is made

Renderer. Node with @napi-rs/canvas (Skia) draws each frame across worker threads. Segments are encoded with ffmpeg and muxed with the audio. Previews render at 1440×1080; the final render is 2880×2160 with 4-sample motion blur.

Judge loop. After each render, a script builds contact sheets of the reference next to the render at 4 fps. Several Sonnet “judge” agents compare them, score sections out of 10 and list the biggest differences. Those notes become the next iteration’s fixes, and the cycle repeats.
